30-day action plan: launch an affiliate SEO site (stay focused)

Last updated: April 11, 2026.

The hardest part in SEO/affiliate isn’t “what to do”. It’s what order to do it in, without going in every direction.

This plan gives you a simple 30-day path. It doesn’t promise revenue in 30 days. It aims for a realistic goal: a clean, structured, measurable site, with your first pages published and a system to iterate.

0) Principles (avoid the traps)

  • 1 niche (at first): otherwise you dilute focus.
  • Max 2 programs: one core + one alternative.
  • 1 replicable money template: you publish faster.
  • Measure early: otherwise you optimize blindly.
  • Link systematically: otherwise pages stay weak.

Week 1: niche + programs + plan

Goal: pick a winnable niche and validate monetization.

  1. Pick 2 niche ideas and score them → keep the best.
  2. Validate 2–3 programs (one core + one alternative).
  3. Define the angle (micro-niche): who it’s for, the problem, the promise.
  4. Content plan: 1 hub + 5 money + 10 supports (rough list).

Full guide to avoid bad choices: choose a niche and pick affiliate programs.

Week 2: structure + pages + tracking

Goal: a clean, indexable, measurable site.

  1. Create the hub → money → support structure (menus, categories, clean URLs).
  2. Create legal pages + affiliate disclosure (trust).
  3. Connect Search Console + GA4 + affiliate click events (minimum).
  4. Prepare 1 replicable “comparison” or “review” template.

For templates, use these: affiliate SEO content templates. For measurement: tracking (GA4/UTM).

Week 3: publish money pages

Goal: publish your first decision pages (the ones that can convert).

  1. Publish 2 money pages (comparison / review / alternatives).
  2. Publish 3 support pages that link to those money pages.
  3. Create/update the hub page to link to money pages.

Priority: clear verdict + table near the top + internal links. Don’t aim for perfection—aim for consistency.

Week 4: indexing + internal links + optimization

Goal: help Google discover pages and improve what converts.

  1. Check indexing (GSC) + fix non-indexed pages.
  2. Add 5–10 internal links (support → money, hub → money).
  3. Improve 1 money page (intro/verdict + table + CTA).
  4. Set a weekly routine (30–60 min).

Detailed method: internal linking.

After 30 days: what to do next

After 30 days, you’re not “done”. You’ve started. The healthiest next steps:

  • Publish 1 money page/week + 2 supports/week.
  • Refresh pages that start ranking (positions 8–20).
  • Reinforce winners (internal links + more supports around them).
  • Test 1 alternative program if needed (without breaking everything).

Conclusion

The “secret” isn’t going fast. It’s having a clear order, a structure, and an iteration loop.
