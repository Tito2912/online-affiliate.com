Method (hands‑on)

Every niche follows the same logic: intents → pages → internal linking → measurement → iteration.

SEO structure (example)

  • 1 “hub” page + money pages (comparisons/guides)
  • Support pages (FAQ, glossary) for long‑tail
  • Simple internal linking: hub → money → support
  • CTAs + disclosure on every page

Measurement & iterations

  • GA4: affiliate click tracking, UTM
  • Search Console: indexing, queries
  • Optimization: titles/meta, CTAs, internal linking
  • Decisions: double down on what converts

Page template

  • Short intro + promise + criteria
  • Table/shortlist + pros/cons
  • Tracked CTAs + visible disclosure
  • FAQ (and schema if enabled)

30‑day checklist

  • Indexing: sitemap + GSC + fixes
  • Content: add new pages
  • Internal links: strengthen clusters that rise
  • Conversion: CTAs, tables, simple tests

When to scale

  • More pages when a niche clicks
  • More programs when it converts
  • More languages when the model works
  • Subscription if you want to iterate without thinking about it

FAQ

Who owns the accounts and the website?

The client, always. Domain, hosting, repositories (GitHub), analytics and admin access are in the client’s name. E-Com Shop can create/configure the accounts on your behalf (with your authorization), then hands over all access and can remove its operator access on request.

Do you create the accounts for me?

Yes. By default, we handle the process end‑to‑end. If a platform requires account‑owner validation (email/SMS/KYC), we guide you through the step (you do it live), then we continue. The domain is purchased/registered in your name (included) without asking for your banking details. For platforms that pay you (affiliate programs), payout details are entered directly with the provider.

Is it really passive income?

The goal is to build an SEO asset: once launched (structure + content + internal linking), it can keep generating traffic and affiliate clicks even when you’re not working on it every day. There is still execution and maintenance (publishing, optimizing, adding programs). No income is guaranteed.

Do you guarantee income?

No. Affiliate marketing/SEO depends on the niche, competition, seasonality, programs, and content volume/quality. The guarantee is the delivery of the scope you paid for, not a financial result.

What if I want more content or a custom design?

You can add a content pack in the configurator. For custom work (agency design, specific features, large volume, link building, ads, etc.), use the special request.

What do you need from me?

A niche choice (or direction), quick validations (niche, domain, languages), and a contact email. Some platforms may require administrative info (address, date/place of birth) for anti‑fraud/KYC: we only ask what’s necessary.

How long does delivery take?

Foundations can be delivered quickly. Timeline depends on the number of pages, languages, current workload and external validations (DNS, platforms, KYC). The configurator shows an estimate, confirmed after payment.

Which niche should I pick?

To start fast, pick a niche with lots of long‑tail (tech, home, DIY, pets). To aim higher (SaaS/lead), potential can be higher but competition too. See the niche ranking.

How long until it can be profitable?

We talk potential, not guarantees. In SEO/affiliate, first conversions often arrive after 2–6 months, and more stable profitability is usually 6–18 months (depending on niche, volume, iterations). We provide indicative scenarios in the niches section.

Do prices vary with the number of pages?

Yes for content (packs 1/5/10/20 pages, per language). And some options (SEO/Schema) are tiered based on the content pack. The other items are mostly one‑shot setup and stay fixed.
